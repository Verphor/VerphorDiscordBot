Verphor Client Discord Bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Verphor Client Discord bot (the Bot), operated by the Verphor team. It does not cover the Minecraft mod, other bots, or Discord's own handling of your information. Contact support@verphor.tech with privacy questions.

Bot data

The Bot currently has no active user-facing features. It does not ask for personal information or keep user records such as message archives, linked accounts, tickets, or payment details. It does not sell or share user records for advertising.

Discord handles your use of Discord, including app authorization, under its Privacy Policy. If you view these documents on GitHub or GitHub Pages, GitHub handles technical information about your visit under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request. The email provider processes the correspondence to deliver and store it. We keep it only as long as necessary to resolve the request or meet legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, account tokens, billing details, or identity documents.

Access, correction, and deletion

To request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold about you in connection with the Bot, email support@verphor.tech. Tell us which information the request concerns. Other rights and the right to complain to a data protection authority may apply depending on your location. Removing the Bot or revoking its authorization does not delete information held independently by Discord.

Updates

Before enabling features that change how the Bot handles personal information, we will update this policy to describe the new data, how it is used and shared, and how long it is kept.
